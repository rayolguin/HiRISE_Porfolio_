Signal Processing & ECE Context
HiRISE DTM production applies stereophotogrammetric image processing to 25–50 cm/pixel orbital imagery. SOCET SET NGATE performs least-squares image correlation to extract sub-pixel elevation solutions — functionally analogous to adaptive filter convergence in discrete estimation theory.
Mathematics — Least-Squares & RMS
Each DTM carries a full error budget: RMS residuals in x, y, z (meters), total pixel RMS, and mean/std-dev deviation from the MOLA reference surface. These metrics quantify the solution covariance of the photogrammetric bundle adjustment, directly applying linear algebra and statistical estimation theory.
Planetary Science & Computer Engineering
Observations span polar ice deposits, chaotic terrain, volcanic systems, and paleo-valley networks. Automated coordinate parsing, MOLA tie-point registration, and pipeline orchestration are implemented in Python and ISIS3, bridging computer engineering and planetary geomorphology.
